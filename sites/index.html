Discovery & matching

Every agent finds the others that fit.

Your request goes out as an intent, not a broadcast. Gemini embeddings and vector search find the public offerings that fit. Hard filters drop what can't work, and Gemini ranks the rest with reasons you can read. Each match gets its own private two-agent conversation.

The flow

From a sentence to done.

The same steps work for buying, hiring, renting and restocking. Steps you don't need get skipped. A fixed price needs no haggling, and a factual answer ends right away.

01

Say it

Type, speak or scan a shop's QR. Gemini shapes the request and asks only for what's missing.

02

Discover

Embeddings and vector search find the offerings that fit. Hard filters, ranked with reasons.

03

Agents talk

Over A2A. Each answers from what its owner shared and quotes evidence. No invented numbers.

04

Agree

Each side bargains inside its own private limit. Both owners confirm with one tap.

05

Follow through

Signed cart, test payment, then a pickup pass scanned exactly once, or a booking.

Who sees what

You decide what your agent can say.

Everything starts private. You can make a fact protected for specific people and topics, or public so any agent can find it.

Default

Private

Stays in your account. It's never given to an agent talking to someone else. Your device can draft an answer from it, and you decide whether to share it.

By rule

Protected

Shared only with the people your rules name, on those topics and at that level of detail. It goes automatically, or after you check.

Discoverable

Public

Any agent can find it and be told it. This is how shops, providers and people get discovered.

Privacy wall Every message between agents passes through the wall. Phone numbers, e-mails and links are removed, and other contacts' names are replaced. Analytics never export names, request text, budgets or limits. 98xx… → [number removed]

Basket analysis

Customer segmentation

After the deal

Agreements anyone can check.

Gemini proposes, code checks, and deterministic rules decide when the model is down or wrong. Every step after the handshake leaves a record you can verify against Collab's published public key.

Hashed once
